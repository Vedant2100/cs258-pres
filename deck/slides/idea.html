Chen et al., NeurIPS 2021

3 / 9

About 1 minute. "The central move is to drop the value function and treat RL like language modeling. A language model completes a prompt. Here the prompt is the return you want plus the states so far, and the completion is the next action. Three contributions: offline RL becomes next-token prediction with no Bellman backups or policy gradients; the return is a prompt you choose at test time; and this simple recipe matches or beats state-of-the-art model-free offline RL on Atari, OpenAI Gym and Key-to-Door."
