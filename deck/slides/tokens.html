Chen et al., NeurIPS 2021

4 / 9

About 50 seconds. "Each timestep becomes three tokens: return-to-go, state, action. Return-to-go is the reward still to come. In this toy episode the rewards are 1, 0 and 2, so working backward from the end, return-to-go is 2, then 2, then 3. Connecting to class: this is the Monte Carlo return G-t with gamma equal to 1; the paper deliberately avoids discounting. Feeding future return instead of past reward is what lets us prompt the model with a goal. Compare with TD: the backward pass still happens, but once, exactly, by summing rewards in hindsight, not learned through backups." Index note if asked: the paper's R̂ₜ includes rₜ itself; Sutton and Barto's Gₜ starts at rₜ₊₁. Same idea, shifted index.
