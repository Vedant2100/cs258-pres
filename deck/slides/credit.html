About 50 seconds. "Key-to-Door tests credit assignment: you must pick up a key in the first room to get a reward in the third. Trained only on random trajectories, DT succeeds 72% of the time with 1K trajectories and 95% with 10K, while CQL stays around 13%. Percent-BC, which clones only the successful episodes, does about as well, which is fair to mention. When all Hopper reward is moved to the end of the episode, DT barely changes and CQL collapses. The paper's attention maps show DT attending to the key pickup and the door."
