← Looking back

1989 ALVINN: behavior cloning, imitate logged actions

Looking forward →

2021 Trajectory Transformer: concurrent; models rewards too, plans with beam search

2021 RvS: a tuned MLP with return conditioning often matches DT

2022 Online DT, Multi-Game DT: online fine-tuning; one model for 41 Atari games

2022 Brandfonbrener et al.; Paster et al.: fails to stitch; misled by luck

2023 Q-learning DT: relabels R̂ with learned Q-values; a backward pass returns, to stitch

Backup

Backup slide for Q&A, not part of the 8 minutes. Left, looking back: behavior cloning, hindsight relabeling, the GPT stack, and the direct ancestors, Upside-Down RL and reward-conditioned policies, which already commanded a policy with a desired return. Also the offline TD methods DT is compared against. Right, looking forward: Trajectory Transformer was concurrent and plans with beam search. RvS showed the transformer isn't essential. Online DT and Multi-Game DT extended it. Brandfonbrener et al. and Paster et al. formalized the limits: no stitching, and stochastic environments reward luck. Q-learning DT patches stitching by relabeling return-to-go with learned Q-values, which brings a learned backward pass back. Elastic DT, also 2023, tackles stitching differently, by adapting how much history the model sees.
